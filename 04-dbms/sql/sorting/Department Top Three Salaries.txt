Link:   https://leetcode.com/problems/department-top-three-salaries/description/?envType=study-plan-v2&envId=top-sql-50


Note:
-> see the use of rank(), dense_rank() and row_number() in ./rank+dense_rank+row_number.txt file




Solution:

select d.name as Department, tmp.name as Employee, Salary from (
    select * , dense_rank() over (partition by departmentId order by Salary desc) as rnk
    from Employee
) as tmp
join Department d
on tmp.departmentId =d.id
where rnk<=3
