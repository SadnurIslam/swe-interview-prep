Link:  https://leetcode.com/problems/find-users-with-valid-e-mails/



note:
=>binary is used to make the comparison case-sensitive, ensuring that only email addresses with the exact domain "leetcode.com" (in lowercase) are considered valid.



Solution: 

select * from Users
where mail regexp '^[A-Za-z]+[A-Za-z0-9_.-]*@leetcode\\.com$'
and mail like binary '%@leetcode.com'
