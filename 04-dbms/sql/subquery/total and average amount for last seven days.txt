Note:
-> second subquery (average_amount) te avg use na kore sum use korte hbe karon
avg sob jog kore total row diye vag kore, ekhetre jodi kono date a transaction
multiple thake tokhn ans vul asbe 
tai sob jog kore 7 diye vag kore dite hbe
-> group by na kore distinct visited_on use korleo accept hbe



select visited_on, (
    select sum(amount) from Customer
    where visited_on between date_sub(c1.visited_on, interval 6 day) and c1.visited_on
) as amount,
(
    select round(sum(amount)/7,2) from Customer
    where visited_on between date_sub(c1.visited_on, interval 6 day) and c1.visited_on
) as average_amount
from Customer c1
where visited_on>=(
    select date_add(min(visited_on),interval 6 day) from customer
)
group by visited_on
order by visited_on
